Welcome to SIHAF

Dedicated to Equipping Young Minds for Lifelong Achievement

At SIHAF K-12 Learning Academy, we operate under the fundamental belief that every student possesses the innate capacity to achieve scholastic excellence when provided with rigorous curriculum, modern technology, and a supportive learning framework.

Our dedicated team of educators and mentors collaborates with families in Shreveport and surrounding Caddo Parish to bridge learning gaps, foster critical thinking in STEM disciplines, and cultivate social-emotional resilience.

STEM & Innovation Focus

Building Tomorrow's Technical Leaders

Our curriculum goes beyond conventional instruction by embedding applied science and technology tools into daily learning modules. Students engage with hands-on projects designed to build practical computing skills.

  • Introductory Computer Science: Python fundamentals, web structures, and algorithmic logic.
  • Robotics & Engineering: Hands-on hardware assembly and logic-driven motor programming.
  • Digital Citizenship: Safe internet practices, media literacy, and data privacy responsibility.
