Comprehensive Privacy Policy

FERPA & COPPA Compliant Data Protection Framework

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Entity Name: SIHAF K-12 LEARNING ACADEMY INC (EIN: 47-1775962)

SIHAF K-12 Learning Academy ("Academy", "we", "us", "our") is deeply committed to maintaining the absolute confidentiality, security, and integrity of personal and educational data collected from students, parents, guardians, educators, and visitors across our campus and digital platforms (including our domain at sihafk12learningacademy.org and associated Google Workspace infrastructure). This comprehensive Privacy Policy explains our data handling practices in compliance with federal laws including the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

1. Educational Records and FERPA Compliance

Under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), parents and legal guardians of minor students, as well as eligible students aged 18 or older, possess defined rights regarding official educational records maintained by SIHAF K-12 Learning Academy. These rights include:

  • Right of Inspection: The right to inspect and review the student's educational records within 45 days of submitting a written request to campus administration.
  • Right of Amendment: The right to request the amendment of educational records that the parent or eligible student believes are inaccurate, misleading, or in violation of privacy rights.
  • Right of Consent: The right to provide written consent prior to the disclosure of personally identifiable information (PII) contained in educational records, except to the extent that FERPA authorizes disclosure without consent (e.g., school officials with legitimate educational interests).

2. Compliance with COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act)

SIHAF K-12 Learning Academy strictly adheres to COPPA requirements regarding online data collection for children under the age of 13. When providing digital accounts or learning management tools (such as Google Workspace for Education tools), the Academy acts as the agent for parents, granting consent for students to participate in secure, educational software environments. We do not collect personal information from children under 13 for commercial or marketing purposes.

3. Scope of Collected Information

We collect only the minimum required information necessary for educational enrollment, academic progress tracking, digital platform delivery, and safety compliance:

  • Student Directory & Academic Data: Student name, date of birth, grade level, attendance records, academic performance scores, and emergency contact details.
  • Parent/Guardian Information: Parent name, physical address, email address, phone number, and verified guardianship documentation.
  • Digital & Technical Identifiers: Secure user accounts (@sihafk12learningacademy.org), technical device logs, IP addresses, and digital platform interaction metrics gathered solely for safety oversight and instructional administration.

4. Use and Sharing of Information

SIHAF K-12 Learning Academy never sells, rents, leases, or trades student or parent data to commercial third parties, advertisers, or data brokers. Collected data is utilized strictly for:

  • Delivering K-12 instruction, academic tutoring, and STEM enrichment programs.
  • Managing user provisioning and access control for Google Workspace collaboration software.
  • Communicating critical school updates, academic progress reports, and emergency notices.
  • Fulfilling legal reporting requirements mandated by Louisiana state educational bodies or federal authorities.

5. Information Security Measures

We enforce robust administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect against unauthorized access, destruction, loss, or disclosure of personal data. Digital systems utilize SSL encryption protocols, multi-factor authentication for administrative staff, strict role-based access permissions, and periodic technical audits.
